How to get a room

Step 1

Finding a Room:

Type the name or location of your desired hostel, rental, hotel, or Airbnb in the search bar.

Alternatively, you can click on the Hotels, Rentals, or Airbnbs cards to browse available units by category.

Step 2

Narrow down your options:

Use the filters to select your room based on location, university, single or double, self contained or by name.

Step 3

View details of desired room:

Click on “Details” button to obtain images and pricing of selected room.

Step 4

Charges:

Click on “Get Contact” button and fill in the required details (your name and phone number)

Follow the prompt on your phone where you will be charged 20,000 UGX.

Step 5

Contact of room:

On successful payment, you will receive the contact details of the custodian or landlord.
